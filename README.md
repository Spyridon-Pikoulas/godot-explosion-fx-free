# Explosion FX Free

**6 animated explosion, fire and smoke effects as PNG sprite sheets, free, for any engine.** Each
comes in three sets: 256 px and 128 px HD, and pixel art drawn at 64 px, not shrunk.

### Want more?

**[Explosion FX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/explosion-fx)** has 60: clusters, ground blasts,
fireballs and burning ground on top of these, and every effect in fire, red, blue, green, purple
and white. Same sheets, same player.

### What's inside

- An **explosion**, a **blast**, a **muzzle** flash, a **fire** and **smoke** in fire colours, and
  a white **puff**. The fire and smoke loop seamlessly.
- Transparent PNG sheets, 8 frames a row, plus an `effects.json` with every frame size, frame
  count, fps, loop and anchor, and a GIF preview of each.
- **Godot 4 player included:** `ExplosionFX.spawn(self, "fire_explosion", enemy.position)`.
  Import steps for Unity and GameMaker in the README.

### Compatibility

- **Any engine** that plays sprite sheets: Unity, GameMaker, Godot, Defold, Construct, Phaser,
  LÖVE, MonoGame.
- The Godot player: Godot **4.3 to 4.7**, tested on both ends and on every renderer.
